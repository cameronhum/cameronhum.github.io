Privacy

CymaVolt, published by Cameron S Humphries. 16 November 2026.

CymaVolt is a bench for guitar-pedal circuits. With no sign-in, a design stays in your browser.

On this device

Projects, saved blocks, and the last design are kept in this browser so they are still there the next time you open CymaVolt. They are not uploaded.

A .pmk file and an exported VST3 are written where you save them, on this computer.

Listening

Play uses the audio already on this computer, a note generated here, or a file you choose. That audio is used while the bench is open. It is not recorded and not sent to Cameron S Humphries.

If you choose this device as the source, the microphone or the audio device stays on this computer.

What this app does not do

There is no account and no sign-in. It does not show ads. There is no payment in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the bug report on the CymaVolt page.
