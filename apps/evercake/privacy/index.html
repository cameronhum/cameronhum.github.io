Privacy

Evercake, published by Cameron S Humphries. 16 November 2026.

Evercake is a birthday calendar for Windows and Android. It does not have an account, and it does not send what you do to Cameron S Humphries or to anyone else.

People

Names, dates, photos, gift ideas, party notes, likes, facts, and reminder settings stay on the device. They are not uploaded.

What this app does not do

It does not show ads, it does not include analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information. There is no sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the bug report on the Evercake page.
