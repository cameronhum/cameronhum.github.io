Evercake

Cameron S Humphries

Evercake is a birthday calendar by Cameron S Humphries for Windows and Android. Home shows who is next. The calendar marks the day. Each person can hold a photo, a gift, and a reminder.

What it can do

Evercake is one place for other people’s days. You add someone once. The app keeps the date, and tells you when it is close.

  • Home shows who is next.
  • Birthday days fill the calendar.
  • Add, edit, and delete a person. You can paste one in.
  • A person can hold a photo, a gift idea, a party note, likes, and facts.
  • Turn a reminder on for a birthday.
  • A 29 February birthday is kept on 28 February in other years.
  • Looks include Soft Sky, Midnight Party, Garden, and Clean Light.
  • Painted scenes include Garden table, Midnight toast, Meadow picnic, Jewel ballroom, Candlelit supper, Starry porch, Rose parlor, and Citrus brunch.

On your device

Evercake runs on Windows and Android. People, photos, notes, and reminders stay on the device. There is no account and no sign-in. Evercake does not show ads.
