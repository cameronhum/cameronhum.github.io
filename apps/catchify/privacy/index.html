Privacy

Catchify, published by Cameron Stewart Humphries. 16 November 2026.

This policy covers the app. The Cymaware website privacy page covers visits to this website.

Catchify is a playlist inbox that talks to Spotify from your browser. There is no Catchify server.

Spotify

You log in with Spotify. After that, Catchify can read the playlists you pick, liked songs, and saved albums, so it can list newer tracks that are not already in those playlists. It writes to Spotify only when you add the tracks you selected to a playlist.

Spotify handles the login. Catchify does not receive your Spotify password.

On this device

The Spotify tokens are stored in this browser so you stay signed in. Log out to delete them from this browser. You can also revoke Catchify in your Spotify account.

What this app does not do

It does not sell what it reads. It does not show ads. It does not build an audience from your listening. There is no payment in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the bug report on the Catchify page.
