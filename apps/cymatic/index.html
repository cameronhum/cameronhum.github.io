Cymatic

Cameron S Humphries

A dish of water that locks into patterns from the sound already playing on your PC, or from a tone in the app. A steady pitch locks the dish. Silence lets the water settle.

Sound

If you choose PC sound, the app reads the audio already playing through your speakers so the dish can follow the pitch and loudness. That audio is used on this computer while the window is open. It is not recorded, not saved, and not transmitted. The built-in tone is generated on this computer.

On your PC

Cymatic runs on 64-bit Windows 10 version 2004 or later, including Windows 11. Colours, surface, and the other controls are saved on this PC. There is no account. It does not show ads and it does not include analytics.
