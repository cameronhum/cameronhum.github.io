Privacy

Cymatic, published by Cameron Stewart Humphries. 8 October 2026.

This policy covers the app. The Cymaware website privacy page covers visits to this website.

Cymatic runs on your Windows PC. It does not have an account, and it does not send what you do to Cameron Stewart Humphries or to anyone else.

Sound

If you choose PC sound, the app reads the audio already playing through your speakers so the dish can follow the pitch and loudness. That audio is used on this computer while the window is open. It is not recorded, not saved, and not transmitted.

The built-in tone is generated on this computer.

Settings

Sliders, colours, and the other controls are saved on this PC so they are still there the next time you open the app. They stay on this PC.

Purchases

If you buy the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the payment. Cameron Stewart Humphries does not receive your card number or other payment details.

What this app does not do

It does not show ads, it does not include analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information. There is no sign-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact on the Cymatic listing in the Microsoft Store.
