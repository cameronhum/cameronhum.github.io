Cloud Shatter

Cameron S Humphries

Coming soon. Not for sale yet.

Cloud Shatter is a side-view arcade dogfight for Windows by Cameron S Humphries, still in development. Fly the sortie, then come back to the hangar and fit the aircraft with the gold and gems you brought home. It is not for sale yet.

What it can do

Cloud Shatter is a side-view fight in the clouds. The aircraft is one body. You pitch with it, fire along the nose, and bring home what the sortie paid. It is for short matches, from a duel up to sixteen aircraft.

  • A match is 1v1, 2v2, 4v4, or a free-for-all of 16. Empty seats are flown for you.
  • The gun, missiles, a heat missile, bombs, and flares. The gun overheats if you hold it. Flares pull a tracking missile off the aircraft.
  • Hits chew pixels out of the sprite. A wing stays on until the pixels holding it to the body are gone.
  • A stall stops the engine. Point the nose down and it comes back when the wings are flying again.
  • The hangar fits the engine, wing, plate, paint, a flag, an attack, support, and a booster that lasts one fight.
  • Mass, thrust, gun armor, blast armor, and roll change with the parts you fit.
  • The shop sells wooden, silver, and gold chests, and exchanges gold and gems. A win can drop a chest.
  • Challenges pay gold, gems, and chests for the day and the week.
  • Gold, gems, and the fitted aircraft stay on this PC.

On this PC

There is no account and no sign-in. The hangar is saved with the game, on the computer where you play. Cloud Shatter does not show ads.

FAQ

What is Cloud Shatter?

Cloud Shatter is an arcade dogfight for Windows by Cameron S Humphries. You fly a sortie, then fit the aircraft, open chests, and take on challenges.

What can Cloud Shatter do?

A match is 1v1, 2v2, 4v4, or a free-for-all of 16. You have a gun, missiles, a heat missile, bombs, and flares. Hits chew the aircraft, and a wing stays on until the piece holding it is gone. The hangar changes the engine, wing, plate, paint, attack, and support. The shop sells chests and exchanges gold and gems. Challenges pay gold, gems, and chests.

Does Cloud Shatter need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. The aircraft, gold, and gems stay on this PC.

Where does the hangar go?

On this PC. Gold, gems, owned parts, and the fitted aircraft are saved with the game. They are not uploaded.

Where can I download Cloud Shatter?

Cloud Shatter is not on the Microsoft Store. It is a Windows game still in development.

How much does Cloud Shatter cost?

Cloud Shatter is not for sale. Gold and gems are spent inside the game. There is no payment.
