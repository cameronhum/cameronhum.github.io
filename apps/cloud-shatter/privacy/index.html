Privacy

Cloud Shatter, published by Cameron Stewart Humphries. 16 November 2026.

This policy covers the app. The Cymaware website privacy page covers visits to this website.

Cloud Shatter is an arcade dogfight for Windows. The hangar stays on the PC where you play.

On this PC

Gold, gems, owned parts, fitted slots, boosters, and chests are saved with the game so they are still there the next time you play. They are not uploaded.

What this game does not do

There is no account and no sign-in. It does not show ads. Gold and gems are inside the game. There is no payment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the bug report on the Cloud Shatter page.
