Privacy

Ambry, published by Cameron S Humphries. 16 November 2026.

Ambry is a meals, pantry, and shopping list. With no sign-in, that list stays in your browser.

On this device

Meals, ingredients, foods, notes, quantities, and expiry dates are saved in this browser so they are still there the next time you open Ambry. Until you sign in, they are not uploaded.

A shared household

Sign-in is optional. You can use email and a password, or Google. After you sign in, Ambry stores the household list, your email, and the names of the people in the household, so someone with the invite code can open the same cupboard. Joining a household switches you to that list.

The shared copy is stored with Google Firebase. Cameron S Humphries does not receive your password. Google handles the Google sign-in.

What this app does not do

It does not show ads and it does not sell the list. There is no payment in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the bug report on the Ambry page.
