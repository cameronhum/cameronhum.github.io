Ambry

by Cameron Stewart Humphries

Coming soon. Not for sale yet.

Ambry is a meals, pantry, and shopping list in development by Cameron Stewart Humphries. Tick the meals you want. It checks them against what the house already has, then lists what you still need to buy. It is not for sale yet.

What it can do

Ambry is the cupboard for the house. One side is the meals. The other is the food you have and the food you still need to get. It is for cooking from what is already there, and shopping only for the rest.

  • Add a meal, give it ingredients and a note, and tick it when you want to cook it.
  • Each ingredient is checked against the pantry. A match is marked have. Anything else is need.
  • Cook next puts the ticked meals in an order, and names what you would still have to buy.
  • To buy gathers the missing ingredients, the amount, and the meals they are for.
  • Add one line to Get, or send the whole to-buy list across.
  • Food is split into Have and Get. Search the names and notes. Filter to do, done, or all.
  • A food can carry a quantity, a note, and an expiry. Anything due in the next few days is called out.
  • The list stays in this browser. Sign in when you want to share one household with a code.

On this device

With no account, meals and foods are saved in this browser and are still there the next time you open Ambry. They are not uploaded.

Sign in with email or Google to keep one household. You get a code. Someone else can join with that code and see the same meals and the same cupboard. Ambry does not show ads.

FAQ

What is Ambry?

Ambry is a meals, pantry, and shopping list by Cameron Stewart Humphries. You tick the meals you want, and it shows what is already in the house and what you still need to buy.

What can Ambry do?

You keep meals and their ingredients, and a list of food you have or still need to get. Ticked meals are ordered by what you can cook next. Missing ingredients become a to-buy list, and you can send them to Get. Each food can have a note, a quantity, and an expiry. Search covers the names and notes. Open, done, and all filter the lists.

Does Ambry need an account?

No. With no sign-in, the meals and the food lists stay in this browser. Sign in with email or Google when you want a household that other people can join with a code.

Where does the list go?

Until you sign in, it stays in this browser. After you sign in, the household list, your email, and the names of members are stored so anyone with the invite code can open the same cupboard.

Where can I download Ambry?

Ambry is not available yet. It is being built to run in the browser, and it is not on the Microsoft Store.

How much does Ambry cost?

Ambry is not for sale. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase.
