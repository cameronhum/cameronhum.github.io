Privacy

While Ago, by Cameron S Humphries. 3 November 2026.

While Ago runs on your Windows PC. It does not have an account, and it does not have a server. It does not send what you log to Cameron S Humphries or to anyone else.

On this PC

The activities you add, the times you log, notes, workspaces, and settings such as light or dark stay on this PC. They are there the next time you open the app. They are not uploaded.

If you export a backup, that is a file you save yourself. While Ago does not send that file anywhere.

What this app does not do

There is no sign-in. While Ago does not include analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information. It does not show ads for now.

Microsoft Store

While Ago is a Microsoft Store app. It is not published yet. When it is listed, Microsoft handles the store listing. If a purchase is offered later, Microsoft processes the payment. Cameron S Humphries does not receive your card number or other payment details.

Removing the app

If you uninstall While Ago, the data the app stored on the device is removed with it. A backup file you already saved somewhere else stays until you delete that file.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go through the bug report on the While Ago page.
