Cameron S Humphries

Cameron S Humphries makes independent software. Cymatic and While Ago are on the Microsoft Store. CymaVolt, Cloud Shatter, Catchify, Ambry, and Evercake are in development.

Apps

Cymatic Real-time Windows cymatics visualizer. Turn music and PC audio into interactive water patterns, without a microphone. Privacy
While Ago Windows activity tracker. See when you last did things, organise routines and habits, and keep your data offline. Privacy

Upcoming

In development, and not for sale yet. Names may still change.

CymaVolt Coming soon Guitar pedal circuit designer. Wire components, hear the circuit in real time, and export pedals for the bench or plugin.
Cloud Shatter Coming soon Arcade sky combat. Sortie through cloud decks, flares, and missiles in a fast Windows dogfight game.
Catchify Coming soon Spotify catch-up. Find newer songs from artists already in your playlists, choose the ones you missed, and add them to a playlist.
Ambry Coming soon Meals, pantry, and a shared shopping list. A cupboard for what the house has and what it still needs.
Evercake Coming soon Birthday calendar for Windows and Android. See who is next, set reminders, and keep photos on each person’s day.
